Build a complete personal dashboard and assistant for Kaleb on iPhone and Mac, continuing the ideas in kleonguerrero209/kaleb-coding-lab. The user wants a full rebuild and autonomous implementation.

Must work while the Mac is OFF. Do not rely on a Mac helper, open browser, or Codex automation as the production background scheduler. Use hosted services for background planning. Preserve fixed existing appointments; calendar writes are limited to app-created blocks unless explicitly approved.

Use Sites building and hosting skills. Keep data in D1, scoped to authenticated user on every request. Never expose credentials to the client or commit them. Do not call a mock or keyword parser a full AI assistant. Do not claim iCloud integration or background notifications verified without live tests. Keep ROADMAP.md accurate. Build and test scheduling, persistence, ownership and error handling before publication.
